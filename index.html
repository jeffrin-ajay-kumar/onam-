THE HEART OF THE FESTIVAL

More Than a Festival

🌼

Onam is a celebration of happiness, abundance, tradition and togetherness. It brings people together regardless of background and fills Kerala with color, music, food and joy.

🌼
01

Pookolam

Beautiful floral designs welcome the festive spirit and turn entrances into colorful works of art.

🛶

Kerala's famous boat races transform its waterways into spectacular celebrations of teamwork and tradition.

🍃
03

Togetherness

At its heart, Onam is about people coming together and sharing happiness.
